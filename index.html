01 / The idea

We learn to become someone.

As we grow up and move through the world, old hurt and social rules can teach us to act differently just to get by. Stimease gives those hidden ways of coping a face: the Camouflagers. They are not all of who we are—just shapes we learned to wear in moments we did not yet know how to understand.

03 / Stimease

Meet the Camouflagers.

Each creature holds a different response we might grow into. They are not labels or villains; they are clues to the feelings beneath the surface, and to the many sides a person can carry.

Slow time in echoes, a silent enigmaEveryone carries more than one self.
Stew

The slow-cooking self: still becoming, one life experience at a time.

Berta

Comfort-seeking and overeating: trying to soothe a feeling that has no words yet.

Drix

Bad temper: a fierce guard that arrives before softer feelings can speak.

Loni

Sadness and shutdown: when everything feels too heavy to reach for.

Kavu

Bravado and boasting: acting larger when you are unsure you belong.

Faelen

The guardian: rest, release, and the chance to return to yourself.

“Slowly cooking
our way through life.”

Stimease is about the selves we gather along the way: shaped by memory, social rules, hurt, humor, and the need to keep going. The Camouflagers help us meet those hard-to-understand moments with curiosity instead of shame.

One day, their stories may grow into animation, clothing, and little worlds other people can make their own.
